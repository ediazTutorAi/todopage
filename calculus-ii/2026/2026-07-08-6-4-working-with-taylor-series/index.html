Binomial Series
For any real number r, the Maclaurin series for \(f(x)=(1+x)^r\) is the binomial series. It converges to f for \(|x|\lt 1\), and we write \[(1+x)^r=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{r}{n}x^n\]\[=1+rx+\frac{r(r-1)}{2!}x^2+\cdots+\frac{r(r-1)\cdots(r-n+1)}{n!}x^n+\cdots\] for \(|x|\lt 1\)
Common Functions Expressed as Taylor Series
FunctionMaclaurin SeriesInterval of Convergence
\(f(x)=\frac{1}{1-x}\)\(\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n\)\(-1\lt x\lt 1\)
\(f(x)=e^x\)\(\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{n!}\)\(-\infty\lt x \lt\infty\)
\(f(x)=sin(x)\)\(\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n\frac{x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}\)\(-\infty\lt x \lt \infty\)
\(f(x)=cos(x)\)\(\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n\frac{x^{2n}}{(2n)!}\)\(-\infty \lt x \lt \infty\)
\(f(x)=ln(1+x)\)\(\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^{n+1}\frac{x^{n}}{n}\)\(-1\leq x\leq 1\)
\(f(x)=tan^{-1}(x)\)\(\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n\frac{x^{2n+1}}{2n+1}\)\(-1\leq x\leq 1\)
\(f(x)=(1+x)^r\)\(\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{r}{n}x^n\)\(-1\lt x\lt 1\)
